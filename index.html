            03 How it works

            1. Observe Open-Meteo wind, rain and weather code for every hub (one batched call; cached, synthetic fallback offline).
            2. Compile Weather, strikes, closures, storm cells and fuel shock become per-hub and per-corridor modifiers.
            3. Search Multi-objective A* over (hub × arrival mode) states with a binary min-heap and an admissible great-circle heuristic.
            4. Compare Fastest, cheapest, greenest and edge-penalised alternates, plus a weight-simplex sweep, filtered to the Pareto set.
            5. Simulate Convoys advance on the sim clock and re-plan at the next hub whenever the context changes.

            04 Glossary

            TEU
            Twenty-foot equivalent unit: one standard container. Costs and CO₂e are per TEU.
            Landed cost
            Line-haul, handling, transfers and surcharges from origin to destination gate.
            Dwell
            Time a box spends in a terminal: berth, yard, customs, gauge change.
            Modal transfer
            A lift between sea, rail and road. It costs hours, dollars and CO₂e.
            Chokepoint
            A strait, canal or border that many corridors depend on: Suez, Bab-el-Mandeb, Malaba.
            Pareto-efficient
            No other route is at least as good on time, cost and carbon and better on one.
            A*
            Best-first search guided by a lower bound on the remaining cost. With an admissible bound it is exact.
            Beaufort
            Wind force 0–12. RouteGuard flags hubs above 45 km/h (about force 7).
            SGR / MGR
            Standard-gauge and metre-gauge railway, e.g. Kenya's Mombasa–Nairobi SGR.
            ICD
            Inland container depot (dry port), such as Nairobi ICD or Naivasha.
            ISO 6346
            Container numbering: owner code, category, serial and a check digit, shown boxed.
            Day / Dusk / Night
            Display palettes modelled on ECDIS (IHO S-52) bridge colour tables.
